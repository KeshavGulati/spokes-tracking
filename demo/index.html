A note on what is visualized +

The mask pixels and colors come from generated snapshots of the real Tracker, Frame, and Spoke objects. Pair costs and accepted matches also come directly from the production matching function. Predicted points are snapped to the image grid only for drawing; the tracker compares their continuous physical coordinates before this visualization step.
